FAQ

Common questions

What iOS version does ScanPro require?
ScanPro requires iOS 16 or later and is fully optimized for iPhone and iPad. It supports the latest hardware including Dynamic Island models and all iPad Pro sizes.
Does ScanPro work without an internet connection?
Yes. All scanning, OCR text extraction, PDF conversion, and signature features work fully offline. An internet connection is only used for optional iCloud sync across your devices.
How do I get help or report an issue?
Reach our support team at support@scanprodocs.app. We respond within 24 to 48 hours on business days. Visit our Support page for troubleshooting guides.
