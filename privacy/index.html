ScanPro Docs ("the App") is operated by Hamza Hatab ("we," "us," or "our"), an independent developer based in Jordan. By downloading or using ScanPro Docs, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

01

Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information (optional): If you create an account, we collect your email address and a password (stored as a secure hash). Account creation is optional and only required for cloud-sync or subscription features.
  • Documents & Images: Photos or files you scan, import, or create within the App are processed locally on your device. If you use optional cloud storage features, documents are uploaded only with your explicit action.
  • Signatures: Signatures you draw or import are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly share or export them.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Apple Analytics: If you have opted in to share diagnostics and usage data with Apple (via your iOS device settings), Apple may share aggregated, anonymized usage and crash data with us through App Store Connect. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Crash Reports: If the App crashes, Apple's platform may collect diagnostic information to help us identify and resolve bugs. This data does not include the content of your documents.

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not read, access, or upload the content of your documents, photos, or scans without your explicit action.
  • We do not collect your precise GPS location.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not collect contacts, call logs, or any data unrelated to the App's core functionality.
02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App's core features (scanning, OCR, PDF export, signatures).
  • Authenticate your account if you choose to create one.
  • Manage your subscription and restore purchases.
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues through Apple's crash reporting.
  • Respond to your support requests sent to our support email.
  • Send important service notices (e.g., policy changes) if you have an account. We do not send marketing emails without your explicit consent.
03

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Apple Inc.: As the platform provider, Apple processes purchase transactions and may provide us with anonymized analytics data. See Apple's Privacy Policy for full details.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by applicable law, court order, or government authority, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.
  • Business Transfers: If ScanPro Docs is acquired or its assets are transferred, user data may be part of that transaction. You will be notified in advance via this page or email (if you have an account).

We never share the content of your scanned documents with any third party.

04

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Apple-provided services. Each is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • Apple App Store & StoreKit: Handles all in-app purchases and subscription management. Apple Privacy Policy ↗
  • Sign in with Apple (if used): Optional authentication method for account creation. Apple Privacy Policy ↗
  • Apple App Store Analytics: Aggregated, anonymized usage data provided to us through App Store Connect based on user consent given to Apple. Apple Privacy Policy ↗

We do not integrate any third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or social media tracking SDKs.

05

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We rely solely on Apple's built-in analytics provided through App Store Connect. This data is aggregated and anonymized by Apple before being shared with us — we cannot identify individual users from this data.

Crash reports may include technical device information (model, OS version, memory state at the time of crash) but never include the content of your personal documents or files.

You can control whether your device shares diagnostics data with Apple and developers at any time by going to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

06

Subscriptions & Purchases (Apple IAP)

ScanPro Docs offers optional premium features through Apple's In-App Purchase (IAP) system. All payment transactions are processed entirely by Apple — we never see, receive, or store your credit card number, Apple ID password, or full billing address.

Apple may share with us a transaction receipt that includes:

  • A unique transaction identifier
  • The product purchased and date of purchase
  • Subscription status (active, expired, or refunded)

This receipt data is used solely to verify your subscription and unlock premium features. It is not used for any marketing purpose. For billing questions, refunds, or subscription management, please visit your Apple ID settings or contact Apple Support directly.

07

Data Retention

  • Local device data: All documents, scans, and signatures stored on your device remain there until you delete them or uninstall the App. We have no access to this data.
  • Account data: If you create an account, your account information is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 8).
  • Analytics & crash logs: Anonymized analytics data provided by Apple is subject to Apple's own retention policies. Any derived insights we record are kept for no longer than 24 months.
  • Support emails: Emails sent to our support address are retained for up to 12 months to handle follow-up inquiries, then permanently deleted.
08

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that inaccurate or incomplete data be corrected.
  • Deletion: Request that your account and associated personal data be deleted. To delete your account, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account within the App, or email us at support@scanprodocs.app.
  • Portability: Request an export of your personal data in a machine-readable format (where technically feasible).
  • Opt-Out of Analytics: Adjust your iOS diagnostics sharing settings at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

We will respond to all verifiable requests within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

09

Children's Privacy

ScanPro Docs is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions, including within the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately at support@scanprodocs.app and we will delete it promptly.

10

International Users

ScanPro Docs is operated by an independent developer based in Jordan. If you access the App from outside Jordan, please be aware that any personal data you provide (such as account information) may be transferred to and processed in countries whose data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

By using the App, you consent to this transfer. We take reasonable steps to ensure your data remains protected in accordance with this policy regardless of where it is processed.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK, our legal bases for processing personal data are: (a) performance of a contract — to provide the App's services to you; (b) legitimate interests — for crash reporting and service improvement; and (c) your consent — where we rely on choices you have made (e.g., analytics opt-in via Apple settings).

11

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited information we hold, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS) for any data communicated to our servers.
  • Secure password hashing using industry-standard algorithms for account credentials.
  • Strict access controls — only the developer has access to any server-side user data.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for any account you create and to keep your iOS device up to date.

Your continued use of ScanPro Docs after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
